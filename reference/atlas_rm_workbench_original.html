Consent & authorisation

Review the terms before activating AI-assisted portfolio monitoring.
New clients complete consent once during onboarding. Existing clients sign in directly and are not asked to consent again in this demo.
IMPORTANT: To help optimise your portfolio against your financial goals, your consent is needed for Atlas Wealth Management’s agentic AI to continuously monitor your portfolio, analyse market developments and prepare adjustment recommendations. Every proposed portfolio adjustment remains subject to review and approval by your Relationship Manager. The AI will not independently execute trades.

What you are consenting to

  • Analysis of portfolio holdings and relevant financial information you have authorised Atlas to use.
  • Continuous or scheduled monitoring of allocation, risk, goal progress and relevant market events.
  • AI-assisted recommendations and explanations for your Relationship Manager.
  • Recording of consent, reviews, approvals and related actions for governance and audit purposes.

Important terms and safeguards

This prototype wording is illustrative, not legal advice or a SEBI-approved agreement. Production wording must be reviewed by Atlas compliance and legal teams and aligned with applicable SEBI regulations, privacy requirements, client mandate, risk profiling, suitability and approved terms. Consent does not guarantee returns or authorise the AI to trade independently.
